What is pu-erh?

A tea from Yunnan, China, that is pressed into cakes and can be aged. Raw (sheng) is fresher and more floral. Ripe (shu) is darker, smoother and earthier.

Raw or ripe: which first?

Many people find ripe easier at the start, and raw brighter and livelier. The “Which pu-erh is for you?” section on the home page can help you choose.

How do I brew it without a gaiwan?

A mug with a strainer works. Rinse the leaves quickly with hot water, then steep for short times, around 10 to 30 seconds, and lengthen slowly with each pour.
